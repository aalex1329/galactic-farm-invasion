# Galactic Farm Invasion v0.2

Godot 4 mobile arcade shooter prototype.

v0.2 adds:
- boss fight every 5th wave
- boss health bar and multi-shot attack
- weapon power-up drops
- extra-life drops
- 5 weapon levels with spread fire
- weapon downgrade after taking damage
- escalating enemy health and firing rate
- scoring and particle explosions

Open project.godot in Godot 4.x.
